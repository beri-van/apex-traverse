Şu an kayıt yalnızca cihazda tutulur; hesap veya bulut kaydı yoktur.

Gizlilik Politikası

English

For questions, suggestions or bug reports, write to us:

apextraverse.help@outlook.com
My purchased gems did not arrive.
Check your internet connection, close and reopen the game, then open the shop. If the problem continues, e-mail us with the order ID from your Apple/Google receipt and we will sort it out.
The game showed an error.
Go to Settings → Error Log → Copy and paste the details into your e-mail. This log stays on your device only.
Can I move my progress to another device?
Not at the moment: progress is saved on the device only, with no account or cloud save.
